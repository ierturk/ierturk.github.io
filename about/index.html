Hi there!

I’m Abe (he/they), and I’m retired. Before that, I was a Software Architect at ICterra, where I worked on embedded software development in telecommunication projects. I’m still a PhD candidate at Ege University.

Some facts about me:

I’m an electrical engineer who specializes in Power Electronics. I have worked at all levels of electrical engineering, from the bottom up, as a technician, engineer, lecturer, and executive. I also received formal education at every level of electrical engineering, and I have an MSc degree.

Specialties

  • Embedded Systems/Software
  • Machine Learning
  • Power Electronics
  • Electric Machinery
  • Modeling and Simulations

Experience

In addition to my previous experience, I worked as a Real Time Linux Software Application Engineer and a Senior Embedded Software Engineer. During this time I built whole embedded systems from scratch using Yocto, Docker, Node JS, C/C++, Qt/QML, FreeRTOS/ThreadX RTOS, embedded RPC and IEEE1588. I used the NXP iMX8m, Jetson Nano and STM32H7 as base platforms. I’m also a contributor to eRPC. You can check it on the eRPC GitHub repo, https://github.com/EmbeddedRPC/erpc.
